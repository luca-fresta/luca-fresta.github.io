From Randomness to Quantum Theory

A Winter Workshop in Probability and Mathematical Physics

15 - 17 February 2027, Rome

About

This workshop will bring together researchers working at the interface of probability, quantum mechanics and quantum field theory, with the aim of fostering interactions across these closely related areas.

The event will take place at Università degli Studi Roma Tre in Rome from 15 to 17 February 2027.
